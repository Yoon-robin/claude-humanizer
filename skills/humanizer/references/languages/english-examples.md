# English — Worked Examples

Companion to `english.md` for the `humanizer` skill: a full before/after piece.
Read it for longer copy or when you're unsure how far a rewrite should go. For a
short fix, `english.md` is enough.

## Product blurb

**Before (AI):**
> In today's fast-paced world, staying focused is harder than ever. That's why we
> built Focus — a robust, seamless, and intuitive timer that blocks distracting
> sites and tracks your deep-work hours, designed to help you unlock your full
> potential. It's not just a timer. It's a whole new way to work. Whether you're a
> student or a CEO, Focus empowers you to do more!

**After (human):**
> Staying focused is hard when everything's fighting for your attention.
> Focus is a timer that actually helps. It blocks distracting sites, tracks your
> deep-work hours, and stays out of your way.

Fixed: the filler opener, the hype stack (robust/seamless/intuitive), "unlock your
full potential," the "it's not just X, it's Y" antithesis, "empowers," the
exclamation, and the em-dash. The "Whether you're a student or a CEO" line is cut
rather than reworded, because it names no real audience. The concrete lines
(blocking sites, tracking deep-work hours) are the source's own features, moved up
front; nothing is added to sound vivid. "Focus," a protected product name, is
untouched.
